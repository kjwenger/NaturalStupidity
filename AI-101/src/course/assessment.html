Academic integrity and AI tools

You may use LLMs, including your own local models, as a tutor: to explain a concept, to check a derivation, or to debug code. You may not submit generated text or code as your own work without saying so. Every lab write-up ends with a short statement of which tools you used and for what. In the exam, no tools are allowed.
